01 — About

Two disciplines, one person.

I'm an English Literature student at Kalyani University who somehow ended up shipping production SaaS applications from a phone. I taught myself to design and build full products — auth, databases, payments, and all — using Supabase and Netlify, because I wanted to build things, not just describe them.

The same instinct shows up in my writing: I care about structure, audience, and whether the thing actually works for the person on the other end. That's true whether I'm writing a beginner's guide to sports betting or building a crisis-response dashboard for counselors reviewing high-priority cases.

Alongside both, I run Zero Coin India, a forensic audit consultancy helping Indian crypto investors with VDA tax compliance.
